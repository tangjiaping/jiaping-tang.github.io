Jiaping Tang is a Ph.D. candidate at the State Key Laboratory of Processors, Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. His research focuses on accelerating gate-level and RTL-level simulations, fault simulation optimization, and software-hardware co-acceleration. He has published papers in leading conferences such as DATE, ICCAD, and ATS.
